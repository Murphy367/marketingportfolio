About

I'm Murphy (Zhiyi) Tang. I studied fashion business management at FIT, so I know how a product is made, how it sells and how it gets talked about.

In New York I grew a brand's Instagram by 70% in a month. In Hangzhou I used multi-channel data to sharpen campaign targeting. My thesis tested, with 396 shoppers, how a fashion brand should respond when it is in trouble. Now I'm at USC studying how design, business and technology fit together.
